Swornsteel

A dark fantasy auto battler for your desk. Arrange items on your pack grid, then watch a pulse sweep each column and fire them in order. Where you place each item decides the fight.

Build it, swear it, forge on

Short runs on your desk, with a long road of Deeds, Oaths and armour behind them.

Pack and pulse combat

Every item fires when the pulse reaches its column. Build around 19 items, 8 relics and 5 set bonuses: Blade, Ward, Rune, Engine and Venom.

The road to the Last Keep

Win 10 fights before you lose 5 lives, against computer-built opponents and bosses from Moonfall Moor to the Last Keep.

Auto Fight

Your hero fights on until you lose, equips spoils that fit or upgrade, and stops before bosses so you can rebuild.

Deeds and Oaths

37 Deeds unlock items, relics, heroes and gear over the long run. Each of the 3 heroes has 10 stacking Oath ranks to conquer.

The Armoury

14 armour sets and 14 weapons that visibly change your hero, plus hearths and titles. Soul Ash buys looks, never power.

The forge and Smithy

Your forge earns Embers in the background, even mid-fight. Spend them on the blacksmith's gear and fill the forge room, right up to a forge cat.

Requirements

  • CORSAIR XENEON EDGE
  • iCUE 5.52 or newer on Windows
  • The Extra Large widget slot, horizontal or vertical; smaller slots show a prompt to switch

Good to know

  • Progress is saved with the placed widget. It survives restarts and iCUE updates, but removing the widget erases it.
  • The forge holds up to 16 Embers (8 hours) until you collect them.
  • Swornsteel has no sound and is English only.
  • One-time purchase. Runs entirely on your PC: no microtransactions, account, internet connection or data collection.
