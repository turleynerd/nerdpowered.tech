Starship Ops Panel

Turn your XENEON EDGE into a full-screen starship operations console. Watch your hardware, control your music, fire Stream Deck actions and sound Red Alert when things run hot.

Get it on the Elgato Marketplace See features

For the CORSAIR XENEON EDGE with iCUE 5.46 or newer on Windows. Sold by Nerd Powered on the Elgato Marketplace.

Everything on one screen

Designed for the Extra Large slot, horizontal or vertical, and configured entirely from iCUE.

Primary Systems

Six sensor slots for CPU and GPU temperatures, loads, fans, pumps and more, each with its own label. Hide a slot and the rest grow to fill the space.

Red & Yellow Alert

Tap to go to Red or Yellow Alert, or pick a sensor and a threshold and let the console do it for you when things run hot.

Command Keys

Eight touch keys backed by a virtual Stream Deck. Assign any Stream Deck action and its icon and title show on the console. Optional: turn it off if you don't use Stream Deck.

Audio Subsystem

Now playing with previous, play/pause and next controls for apps that report to Windows media controls.

Stardate & calendar

A large local clock, a live stardate and the whole month at a glance, with today highlighted.

Dockable stations

Tap Sensors, Audio or Command on the sidebar to fold a panel away; the others slide over to use the space. Four color palettes: Classic, Blue, Red and Yellow.

In action

Requirements

  • CORSAIR XENEON EDGE touchscreen
  • iCUE 5.46 or newer on Windows
  • Designed for the Extra Large widget slot, horizontal or vertical
  • Optional: the Elgato Stream Deck app for Command Keys

Good to know

  • Media info comes from Windows media controls. Apps that don't report to them, such as the Pandora desktop app, won't show a song.
  • Like every touch-enabled iCUE widget, the console receives the touches on it, so iCUE's swipe-to-change-page gesture doesn't work on top of it.
  • Runs entirely on your PC. No accounts, no internet connection, no data collection.
