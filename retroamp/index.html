RetroAmp

The classic desktop music player, on your XENEON EDGE. Internet radio, your own music, a real 10-band equalizer, a live visualizer and thousands of classic Winamp 2 skins.

Get it on the Elgato Marketplace See features

For the CORSAIR XENEON EDGE with iCUE 5.46 or newer on Windows. Sold by Nerd Powered on the Elgato Marketplace.

A whole music player on one screen

Player, Equalizer, Playlist and Visualizer windows, drawn pixel for pixel like the classic and configured from iCUE.

Internet radio

All of SomaFM built in with live now-playing, plus search across thousands of stations worldwide, or paste any stream URL.

Your own music

Browse the folders on your PC right on the screen, add songs or whole folders, and keep up to 12 saved playlists.

10-band equalizer

Preamp, ten bands, a live EQ curve and presets, working on radio and your own music.

Live visualizer

Spectrum bars with falling peaks or an oscilloscope, driven by the actual audio. Tap to switch styles.

Classic skins

An original built-in skin, plus classic Winamp 2 skins from the Winamp Skin Museum: pick, search or paste a link.

Fits any slot

The windows rearrange to fill Extra Large, Large or vertical slots. Close any of them with X, and the rest fill the screen.

In action

Requirements

  • CORSAIR XENEON EDGE touchscreen
  • iCUE 5.46 or newer on Windows
  • An internet connection for radio, station search and skins; your own music plays offline

Good to know

  • The equalizer and visualizer work on radio and your own music. For other PC apps, iCUE only shares the song name and controls.
  • RetroAmp only reads the folders you browse to. Playlists and settings stay on your PC: no account, no tracking.
  • Like every touch-enabled iCUE widget, RetroAmp receives the touches on it, so iCUE's swipe-to-change-page gesture doesn't work on top of it.
