Christmas Eve on your desk

From the first countdown in December to Christmas morning, with your own family in it.

Christmas countdown

Days, hours and sleeps to go, workshop news all December, and a sleigh launch countdown on Christmas Eve morning.

Live tracking

Follow the sleigh across 425 cities on NASA's night-lights map, each reached around local midnight, with presents and miles counting all night.

Your house is a stop

See how far away Santa is and when he'll reach your house. It's set from your PC's time zone; search for your city to change it.

Snow where it's snowing

Live weather along the route on Christmas Eve: snow falls on the map as Santa flies through, with each city's conditions and temperature.

Messages for your kids

Add up to six kids. Santa, Mrs. Claus, the elves and Comet write to each by name. No gift promises, and kind words for everyone.

Holiday themes

Christmas Tree, Candy Cane, Christmas Plaid or North Pole, in every slot from Small to Extra Large, horizontal or vertical.

In action

Requirements

  • CORSAIR XENEON EDGE
  • iCUE 5.46 or newer on Windows
  • An internet connection for the map and live weather; offline, it switches to a radar map with built-in weather

Good to know

  • Turn on Custom Style in the widget's settings to get the full holiday theme, then pick a Theme.
  • Kids' names, wishes and settings stay on your PC. No account, no ads, no analytics.
  • Live weather requests contain only the locations of the cities just ahead of Santa, never your home city.
  • Santa's route is a simulation, not real flight data. Set Tracker Mode to a demo to see it before Christmas Eve.
